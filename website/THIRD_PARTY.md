# Third-party material

The static Astro + Tailwind foundation and section-wrapper pattern are adapted
from [AstroWind](https://github.com/arthelokyo/astrowind), commit
`097994144d9fcbba2e45a100650bd0ff8616ffd3` (MIT). Its license is retained in
`licenses/AstroWind-LICENSE.md`. Blog, analytics, remote embeds, CMS integrations
and the template's sample content are omitted. Page layouts, diagrams, copy and
interactions are custom to MS-Agent.

Inter is distributed by `@fontsource-variable/inter` under the SIL Open Font
License; its license is included in that npm package. Chinese typography uses
the viewer's system fonts. The MS-Agent logo and favicon come from this
repository's WebUI. See `public/media/manifest.json` for demonstration provenance.
